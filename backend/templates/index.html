✧  Research Status

Independent modality predictions

Audio · Drawing · ECG · MRI

Four separate model pipelines are available. Patient-level multimodal fusion is a future research stage, not an active diagnostic output.
ⓘ  Research limitations   Current classifiers produce separate modality predictions. A meaningful patient-level multimodal result requires verified participant-level links across modalities, sufficient matched samples, and a trained and validated fusion model. This prototype is for research use only, not clinical diagnosis.
